---
name: Choice integration decisions
description: User-approved boundaries for bringing Choice workflows into the Invitation Homes project.
---

Do not import property records from Choice. Use Choice's workflow patterns, but retain Invitation Homes' approved brand, operating rules, and legal terms. Payment tracking and screening are permanent manual staff-recorded workflows (Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, Card, ACH receipts); third-party automated payment APIs and automated screening provider integrations are permanently excluded from this project.

**Why:** The user explicitly established permanent manual operations for payments and screening reviews, rejecting any future payment or screening API integrations.

**How to apply:** Use authenticated staff actions with verified evidence/reference fields and immutable audit history. The system permanently operates on manual staff verification and logging for payments and tenant screening. Do not carry over Choice-specific fees or unapproved legal copy.
