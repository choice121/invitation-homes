---
name: Choice integration decisions
description: User-approved boundaries for bringing Choice workflows into the Invitation Homes project.
---

Do not import property records from Choice. Use Choice's workflow patterns, but retain Invitation Homes' approved brand, operating rules, and legal terms. For the first release, payment and screening are manual staff-recorded steps only; the site must not imply it processed a payment or ran a screening check.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose Choice-style manual operations instead of waiting for payment and screening provider integrations, while keeping Choice property data out of this project.

**How to apply:** Use authenticated staff actions with evidence/reference fields and audit history. Keep provider-confirmed status distinct from staff-recorded status. Do not carry over Choice-specific fees, payment methods, screening criteria, or legal copy without approval.
